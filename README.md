# VacuumShulkers
This plugins adds an awesome new feature to shulker boxes. While there are like a ton of plugins that allow you to open shulker boxes directly from your inventory, VacuumShulkers allows you to combine shulker boxes with a piece of paper and any other items on a crafting table. The shulker box will not automatically pick up all matching items that the player picks up.

### Example
You are mining. Your inventory gets filled with cobblestone. You could just place a shulker box on the ground and put all the cobblestone inside, then continue mining.

With VacuumShulkers, just combine a shulker box with cobblestone and a piece of paper. The shulker box will now automatically pickup all cobblestone that would normally fill up your inventory space.

You can have as many VacuumShulkers in your inventory as you like. VacuumShulker will just fill the next one once the first one is full.

You can add up to seven items to a shulker box. You can also just remove the paper from the shulker box and put it into another once the first one is full.
